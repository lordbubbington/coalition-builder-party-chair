The '''120th United States Congress''' is the upcoming meeting of the [[United States Congress|legislative branch of the United States federal government]], composed of the [[United States Senate]] and the [[United States House of Representatives]]. It will convene on January 3, 2027, during the final two years of [[Donald Trump]]'s [[Second presidency of Donald Trump|second presidency]].

The [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic Party]] won a majority in both chambers in the [[2026 United States elections|November 3, 2026 midterm elections]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Browning |first=Kellen |last2=Griffin |first2=Anna |last3=Klavens |first3=Alex |last4=Harmon |first4=Amy |last5=Yoon |first5=John |last6=Adelson |first6=Jeff |last7=Govindarao |first7=Sejal |last8=Browning |first8=Kellen |last9=Zhang |first9=Christine |date=2026-11-03 |title=Democrats Seize Control of Congress as Voters Rebuke Trump |url=https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/11/03/us/midterms-2026-election-day-trump |access-date=2026-11-04 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

The numbers refer to their [[Classes of United States senators|Senate classes]]. All class 2 seats were contested in the [[2026 United States Senate elections|November 2026 elections]]. In this Congress, class 2 means their term will commence in the current Congress, requiring re-election in 2032; class 3 means their term ends with this Congress, requiring [[2028 United States Senate elections|re-election in 2028]]; and class 1 means their term began in the last Congress, requiring [[2030 United States Senate elections|re-election in 2030]].
